BlueBridge feature map and useful delivery packages

First feature-mapping proposal · 9 October 2026 · responsibilities and record homes remain provisional

The user-reviewed job list is the working basis. Each feature has a minimum version, later increments, prerequisites, and an acceptance example.

A prerequisite must be available. An existing tool or manual process can supply it while internal capability grows. Supplier coverage and delivery dates have not been established.

The first P0/P1/P2 estimation pass assumes a fresh build. The demo supplies design and rough timing references, with no code-reuse saving.

Recommended first useful slice

One transcript or text source becomes reviewed needs and requirements, with source support, confirmed links, saved target records, and correction feedback.

  1. Capture the source and resolve material questions.
  2. Draft cited needs, check/revise, independently inspect, and review them.
  3. Derive requirements from reviewed needs and relevant risk/task context.
  4. Check/revise and independently inspect the requirements, then record human decisions and confirmed links.
  5. Save accepted outputs to the chosen evidence home and retain corrections.

Manual context, independent human checking, and manual transfer can start this workflow. Live connectors can arrive separately. Change review can start first if existing records and traces make that the more useful workflow.

P1 · Turn one source into reviewed needs and requirements

A person captures a transcript, resolves questions, obtains cited candidate needs/requirements, reviews them and their links, and saves accepted outputs to the chosen home.

Features grouped here

CAP-06, CAP-07, CAP-10, CAP-11, CAP-12, CAP-13, CAP-14, CAP-18

Minimum outcome

CAP-06, CAP-10, CAP-11, CAP-12, CAP-13, CAP-14, CAP-18, CAP-35, CAP-38

Completion gate

Complete the flow on a representative source; an independent check finds a deliberately unsupported statement; revisions receive fresh checks; accepted target IDs, citations, decisions, and correction reasons are retrievable.

Sequencing

Drafting, checking, independent scrutiny, human acceptance, usable links, persistence/exchange, and feedback are required together for this promised outcome. AI extraction by itself is a smaller assistance feature.

Later increments

CAP-07 document formats are optional; live Slack/meeting capture can be added independently through P-IN.

Effort drivers; dates remain open

Main drivers: fresh application/storage setup, prompt quality, citation reliability, reviewer workflow, and target-home exchange.

CAP-01 Record scope, evidence homes, and decision responsibilities

Supports J16 · P0

Inputs: SRC, SCOPE, METHOD. Outputs: SCOPE, REVIEW.

Minimum useful version

A recorded agreement names product scope, record types, authoritative homes, link meanings, responsible people, and applicable checks.

Later capability

Configurable scope/role profiles and rules selected for each engagement.

Hard prerequisites

Agree the first scope.

External prerequisites

Responsible BBT/client owners must agree the first scope and permitted decision roles.

Acceptance example

A requirement has one agreed home and named author/reviewer; the team can identify who resolves an ownership conflict.

Documented demo precedent

Product rules and an authority explanation are documented; engagement-level configuration is a broader proposal.
