J01 Capture information with a usable source reference
Actor
Meeting participant, product lead, engineer, or source owner
Trigger
A discussion, document, note, or new source revision contains product information.
Inputs
SRC: Source revisions
Outputs
SRC: Source revisions; CLAR: Clarification and decision

Tasks

  1. Capture the relevant thread, transcript, note, or document revision.
  2. Record origin, author/date where available, revision, and exact locators.
  3. Identify missing context, ambiguity, and candidate decisions for follow-up.

Decision

A source owner confirms usable capture; candidate statements still need their own review.

Handoff

J02, J03, J05, J06, or J09 receives the relevant source and questions.

When something changes

A new source revision raises J13 drift review; earlier interpretations retain their original citation.

Inner loop

Check extraction completeness, locators, duplicates, and structured output.

Outer loop

Inspect the original source for omitted qualifiers, disagreements, or unsupported interpretation.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture extraction errors, missed decisions, and manual redirection.

Friction hypothesis

Decisions may be reconstructed later from scattered conversations.

Measure

Capture effort and the proportion of outputs with usable exact source references.

J02 Agree the user need and its intended-use context
Actor
Product owner with user, clinical, or domain reviewers
Trigger
New evidence, an unresolved clarification, or a change affects what users need.
Inputs
SRC: Source revisions; SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; CLAR: Clarification and decision; USE: Use and usability evidence
Outputs
NEED: User need; CLAIM: Product / clinical claim; CLAR: Clarification and decision; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Identify the user, problem, use environment, and desired outcome.
  2. Resolve ambiguity with the responsible person.
  3. Record proposed product claims separately from user needs; supporting evidence is reviewed in J08.
  4. Review each need and retain its source and rationale.

Decision

The designated need owner/reviewer accepts, revises, rejects, or defers the specific version.

Handoff

J03 derives requirements; J08 defines validation against the need.

When something changes

A changed need prompts review of derived requirements, use-related risks, validation, and claims through J09.

Inner loop

Check clear actor/outcome, source support, duplicates, and unresolved scope.

Outer loop

Challenge interpretation against underlying sources, intended use, and existing needs.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture reviewer rewrites and rejected or unsupported needs.

Friction hypothesis

People may confuse a discussion suggestion with an agreed need.

Measure

Time to an accepted need and reviewer correction effort.

J03 Translate needs and controls into testable requirements
Actor
Systems, software, hardware, or product engineer
Trigger
A reviewed need, control, constraint, or parent requirement needs implementation detail.
Inputs
NEED: User need; SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; CLAR: Clarification and decision; CTRL: Risk control; USE: Use and usability evidence; SYS: Product / system requirement; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Derive product/system requirements and decompose them into software, hardware, UI, and labelling requirements.
  2. Specify measurable acceptance criteria, allocation, and source/parent links.
  3. Review requirement versions and proposed links separately.

Decision

The assigned discipline reviewer accepts the requirement and its links. Parent acceptance does not accept children.

Handoff

J04 uses approved design inputs; J07 plans verification; J12 uses the exact versions in specifications.

When something changes

A changed criterion or allocation prompts design, control, test, and label review through J09.

Inner loop

Check measurability, schema, units, source support, and allowed link types; revise within a bounded loop.

Outer loop

Independently inspect consistency with needs, risk controls, related requirements, and testability.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture rejected requirements, ambiguous criteria, missing links, and recurring reviewer edits.

Friction hypothesis

Requirements may be duplicated in tickets or rewritten retrospectively.

Measure

First-pass acceptance, correction effort, and unresolved source/trace gaps.

J04 Connect intended design, implementation work, and what was built
Actor
Architect and developer, including coding agents under an assigned owner
Trigger
A requirement is allocated, a design changes, or implementation/PR evidence becomes available.
Inputs
SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; CTRL: Risk control; ARCH: Architecture and components; DESIGN: Detailed design; WORK: Implementation work item; CODE: Implementation and build; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
ARCH: Architecture and components; DESIGN: Detailed design; WORK: Implementation work item; CODE: Implementation and build; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Allocate requirements to components, interfaces, and detailed design.
  2. Create implementation tasks that reference controlled IDs.
  3. Capture authored design rationale and as-built observations tied to exact commits and PRs.
  4. Review mismatches between intended design and implementation.

Decision

Design reviewer owns design acceptance; repository reviewers own PR decisions. Neither decision automatically approves the other record.

Handoff

J07 consumes exact design and build references; J09 receives design/implementation mismatches.

When something changes

Code, interface, dependency, or task changes prompt review of the associated design, requirements, risk, and tests.

Inner loop

Check local design/code constraints, referenced IDs, and local tests.

Outer loop

Inspect requirement and control coverage, component boundaries, and configuration consistency beyond the local change.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture missed allocations, design/code divergence, PR corrections, and agent redirection.

Friction hypothesis

The reason for a code change may remain in a PR while design evidence becomes stale.

Measure

Effort to reconstruct requirement-to-build evidence and unresolved design/implementation mismatches.

J05 Analyse risk, define controls, and assess residual risk
Actor
Risk owner with engineering, clinical, security, usability, and QA reviewers
Trigger
Scope/design changes, a hazard is identified, or control/test evidence changes.
Inputs
SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; NEED: User need; ARCH: Architecture and components; CODE: Implementation and build; USE: Use and usability evidence; ML: Dataset and model evidence, if applicable; HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; RUN: Verification execution and results; VR: Validation and clinical results; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; CTRL: Risk control; RISK: Residual-risk decision; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Record hazards, causes, assumptions, and the relevant risk analyses, including security and dependencies where applicable.
  2. Define controls and translate them into requirement/design inputs.
  3. Define how implementation and effectiveness will be checked.
  4. Evaluate residual risk against current evidence and record the responsible decision.

Decision

The named risk authority records acceptance or further work for the relevant product state.

Handoff

J03/J04 implement controls; J07 verifies them; J08 contributes use/clinical evidence; J11/J12 use risk decisions and disclosures.

When something changes

Changed assumptions, controls, failures, or use conditions trigger risk reassessment and linked follow-up.

Inner loop

Check required fields, criteria, control references, and evidence versions.

Outer loop

Challenge missing hazards, ineffective controls, conflicting requirements, and unsupported residual-risk conclusions.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture risk-review changes, missing hazards, and recurring control-evidence gaps.

Friction hypothesis

Risk records may lag behind implementation and test changes.

Measure

Time to assess a change and the number/type of unresolved control-evidence gaps.

J06 Connect user tasks, interface design, and usability evidence
Actor
Designer and usability lead with product and risk reviewers
Trigger
A task, user profile, interface, or use-related risk changes.
Inputs
SRC: Source revisions; SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; NEED: User need; USE: Use and usability evidence; HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; CODE: Implementation and build
Outputs
USE: Use and usability evidence; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; VP: Validation plan / protocol; VR: Validation and clinical results; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Describe user profiles, environment, tasks, and critical tasks.
  2. Use task/risk evidence to define UI requirements.
  3. Record formative evaluations and resulting changes.
  4. Connect summative evaluation plans/results to critical tasks and needs.

Decision

Assigned design/usability reviewers accept interpretations and required changes; risk acceptance has its own owner.

Handoff

J02/J03 receive task evidence; J05 receives use-related risk; J08 receives summative validation evidence.

When something changes

Changed users, tasks, interface, or use errors trigger requirement, risk, and evaluation review.

Inner loop

Check task IDs, design versions, critical-task links, and study/result structure.

Outer loop

Inspect whether tasks and use-related controls are covered by the UI design and evaluation scope.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture missing tasks, repeated use errors, and study/document rework.

Friction hypothesis

Task analyses, UI designs, and evaluation results may be maintained separately.

Measure

Effort to trace a critical task through UI requirements to evaluated outcomes.

J07 Plan verification and preserve results at every V-model level
Actor
Test engineer or developer with assigned verification reviewer
Trigger
Requirements/design need tests, a build is ready, or a change requires regression.
Inputs
SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; ARCH: Architecture and components; DESIGN: Detailed design; CTRL: Risk control; CODE: Implementation and build; TEST: Verification plan / test case; CHANGE: Change and impact work; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
TEST: Verification plan / test case; RUN: Verification execution and results; DEFECT: Anomaly / defect; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Plan unit tests against detailed design, integration tests against components/interfaces, subsystem tests against allocated requirements, and system tests against system requirements.
  2. Record test identity, plan version, acceptance criteria, configuration, and traced items.
  3. Execute against an exact build and retain raw results and attachments.
  4. Review results, record anomalies, and determine relevant regression work.

Decision

The assigned reviewer disposes of each execution and its applicability. A passing run alone does not approve a release.

Handoff

J05 reviews control evidence; J08 references the verified configuration; J11 consumes applicable results and dispositions.

When something changes

A changed test, requirement, build, environment, or failed execution prompts freshness, regression, and impact review.

Inner loop

Check test schema, criteria, configuration, raw-result integrity, and local failures.

Outer loop

Independently inspect requirement/control coverage, missing tests, stale runs, and consistency across levels.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture verification failures, missing coverage, unreliable checks, and incorrect execution associations.

Friction hypothesis

A test result may be available without a reliable link to the requirement and release version.

Measure

Coverage gaps, stale evidence, result-review effort, and missed required regression.

J08 Validate user needs and support applicable product claims
Actor
Validation, usability, or clinical lead with assigned reviewers
Trigger
A product version is evaluated, a need/claim changes, or new study evidence arrives.
Inputs
NEED: User need; SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; USE: Use and usability evidence; CLAIM: Product / clinical claim; VP: Validation plan / protocol; CODE: Implementation and build; RUN: Verification execution and results; ML: Dataset and model evidence, if applicable; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
VP: Validation plan / protocol; VR: Validation and clinical results; CLAIM: Product / clinical claim; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Define representative needs, populations, scenarios, critical tasks, and applicable claims.
  2. Specify criteria and the exact evaluation configuration.
  3. Record results per need/claim, limitations, and unresolved gaps.
  4. Review conclusions and route failures back to needs, design, risk, or evidence collection.

Decision

Assigned reviewers assess whether the evidence supports the specific need or claim. Verification success does not establish validation.

Handoff

J05 uses relevant results for risk decisions; J11/J12 use reviewed conclusions and limitations.

When something changes

A changed need, claim, population, interface, model, or evaluated configuration prompts validation-scope review.

Inner loop

Check protocol/result association, criteria, population, and configuration references.

Outer loop

Challenge representativeness, uncovered needs/critical tasks, claim support, and interpretation of failures.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture validation failures, unsupported claims, study corrections, and repeated evidence gaps.

Friction hypothesis

Validation conclusions may be stored as reports with weak links to individual needs.

Measure

Effort to establish per-need/claim support and unresolved validation gaps.

J09 Determine and resolve the consequences of a change
Actor
Change author and reviewers from affected disciplines
Trigger
A significant source, requirement, design, code, risk, test, label, or relationship changes.
Inputs
SRC: Source revisions; CHANGE: Change and impact work; BASE: Reviewed baseline; NEED: User need; SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; ARCH: Architecture and components; CODE: Implementation and build; HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; CTRL: Risk control; TEST: Verification plan / test case; RUN: Verification execution and results; VR: Validation and clinical results; LABEL: Labelling and instructions for use; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
CHANGE: Change and impact work; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision; WORK: Implementation work item

Tasks

  1. Identify the old state, proposed state, reason, and change scope.
  2. Inspect known links and independently look for missing relationships or consequences.
  3. Assign updates, relinking, retesting, and reviews to owners.
  4. Resolve or explicitly disposition findings before advancing the changed records.

Decision

Affected reviewers decide which impacts are real and what follow-up is required; the author does not approve the agent's conclusions by default.

Handoff

J02 through J08 and J12 receive named follow-up work; J10 receives the reviewed candidate state.

When something changes

A candidate edit or new external revision makes version-bound checks and decisions subject to freshness review.

Inner loop

Check the proposed change and its known direct relationships.

Outer loop

Independently interrogate impacted requirements, hazards, controls, tests, labels, configuration, and potentially absent links.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture missed impacts, false alarms, reviewer additions, and manually redirected analysis.

Friction hypothesis

People may rely on memory to identify impacts across disconnected records.

Measure

Impact-review effort, missed consequential impacts, and unhelpful findings.

J10 Establish a coherent reviewed baseline
Actor
Design reviewers and designated baseline authority
Trigger
A change or review package has a candidate evidence state ready for a decision.
Inputs
CHANGE: Change and impact work; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision; NEED: User need; SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; ARCH: Architecture and components; DESIGN: Detailed design; HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; CTRL: Risk control; USE: Use and usability evidence; TEST: Verification plan / test case; ML: Dataset and model evidence, if applicable; CLAIM: Product / clinical claim; LABEL: Labelling and instructions for use; LINK: Trace relationship
Outputs
BASE: Reviewed baseline; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Select exact evidence and relationship versions for the proposed scope.
  2. Check consistency, completeness, current review decisions, and unresolved issues.
  3. Return work for revision or explicitly disposition it.
  4. Record the baseline decision and retain the previous baseline.

Decision

The designated authority accepts the named evidence state. Baseline acceptance and release approval remain separate.

Handoff

J12 renders controlled views; J11 references the baseline in release/transfer work; J09 uses it as the comparison state.

When something changes

New candidate versions require relevant checks and reviews to run again against that state.

Inner loop

Check membership, IDs, version freshness, and known consistency rules.

Outer loop

Independently inspect package-wide consistency and whether unresolved work changes the decision.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture returned packages, stale decisions, and repeated coherence findings.

Friction hypothesis

Different documents may describe different versions of the product.

Measure

Baseline-review effort and unresolved or recurring configuration inconsistencies.

J11 Establish release or transfer readiness and record the decision
Actor
QA and designated release/transfer owner
Trigger
A named baseline and implementation version are proposed for release or transfer.
Inputs
BASE: Reviewed baseline; CODE: Implementation and build; RUN: Verification execution and results; VR: Validation and clinical results; RISK: Residual-risk decision; LABEL: Labelling and instructions for use; DEFECT: Anomaly / defect; DOC: Document snapshot / package; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision
Outputs
REL: Release / transfer record; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Assemble applicable current verification, validation, risk, labels, anomalies, and output evidence.
  2. Check that the evidence applies to the same proposed product/build state.
  3. Resolve readiness findings and obtain the required distinct decisions.
  4. Record release/transfer scope and retain the exact evidence references.

Decision

The designated release/transfer authority records the final decision according to the chosen process.

Handoff

Operations or the receiving team uses the release record; J13 monitors later changes and feedback.

When something changes

Changed supporting evidence requires readiness and decision freshness review; historical release evidence stays identifiable.

Inner loop

Check required records, file integrity, configuration, and decision freshness.

Outer loop

Inspect cross-package coverage, residual risks, validation limitations, open anomalies, and configuration consistency.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture readiness blockers, missing support, and recurring release-package rework.

Friction hypothesis

People may manually reconcile test reports, code versions, labels, and approval documents.

Measure

Assembly/review effort and late discoveries of missing or mismatched evidence.

J12 Maintain labelling, produce document views, and answer evidence questions
Actor
Evidence or labelling author, reviewer, or QA/RA reader
Trigger
A label, specification, report, trace matrix, design-review package, or evidence answer is needed.
Inputs
BASE: Reviewed baseline; NEED: User need; SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; ARCH: Architecture and components; HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; CTRL: Risk control; RUN: Verification execution and results; VR: Validation and clinical results; RISK: Residual-risk decision; CLAIM: Product / clinical claim; LABEL: Labelling and instructions for use; LINK: Trace relationship; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision
Outputs
LABEL: Labelling and instructions for use; DOC: Document snapshot / package; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Author or revise controlled labelling and instructions for use from requirements, supported claims, limitations, and risk disclosures.
  2. Select the relevant baseline and evidence scope.
  3. Render or assemble a specification, report, matrix, or package using a named template.
  4. Preserve exact source membership and review the output.
  5. Answer a traceability question with references to the underlying records.

Decision

The assigned document/package reviewer accepts the projection and completeness; rendering does not create new product truth.

Handoff

Reviewers, release owners, clients, or other readers receive a version-identifiable output.

When something changes

Changed baseline or template creates a new output and an explainable difference; edits to exported content return through controlled review.

Inner loop

Check membership, layout, references, template version, and required sections.

Outer loop

Inspect completeness and consistency with the underlying evidence, including meaningful omissions.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture recurring document edits, template omissions, and misleading evidence presentation.

Friction hypothesis

Manual copying and reconciliation may consume time and introduce divergence.

Measure

Output-preparation effort, repeated edits, and time to answer a cited evidence question.

J13 Detect drift and route ongoing observations into review
Actor
Source and record owners with affected reviewers
Trigger
A source revision, ticket, commit, test run, defect, or post-release observation arrives.
Inputs
SRC: Source revisions; WORK: Implementation work item; CODE: Implementation and build; RUN: Verification execution and results; DEFECT: Anomaly / defect; REL: Release / transfer record; BASE: Reviewed baseline
Outputs
SRC: Source revisions; CHANGE: Change and impact work; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Capture the observation at an exact external revision.
  2. Compare it with the version previously used by evidence.
  3. Distinguish duplicates, irrelevant updates, and meaningful changes.
  4. Route meaningful drift into J09 with the relevant records and owners.

Decision

The responsible person dispositions drift; external activity does not automatically rewrite controlled evidence.

Handoff

J09 handles product consequences; J15 receives capture and routing failures.

When something changes

Repeated capture of the same revision should converge; lost or failed capture remains visible for recovery.

Inner loop

Check source identity, revision ordering, duplicates, and capture completeness.

Outer loop

Inspect whether material changes were missed or incorrectly associated with controlled records.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture missed/stale observations, duplicate work, routing errors, and recurring drift.

Friction hypothesis

Evidence may fall behind work in developer tools, particularly as AI accelerates changes.

Measure

Time from observation to disposition, missed material drift, and duplicate review work.

J14 Maintain dataset and model evidence where product scope requires it
Actor
ML lead with test, risk, and domain reviewers
Trigger
A dataset, model, training process, performance criterion, or permitted change scope changes.
Inputs
SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; SRC: Source revisions; SYS: Product / system requirement; SUB: Software / hardware / UI / labelling requirement; HAZ: Hazard and risk analysis; ML: Dataset and model evidence, if applicable; TEST: Verification plan / test case; RUN: Verification execution and results; VR: Validation and clinical results
Outputs
ML: Dataset and model evidence, if applicable; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Record dataset origins, versions, partitions, and test-set independence/lock.
  2. Record model/training configuration and the exact artifact used in a build.
  3. Associate performance and subgroup evidence with the intended use and predefined criteria.
  4. Review model/data changes alongside requirements, risk, verification, and validation.

Decision

Named ML/domain reviewers decide the evidence interpretation; risk and release decisions retain their own owners.

Handoff

J04 uses the model artifact; J05, J07, and J08 use its risk and performance evidence; J11 uses the exact released configuration.

When something changes

Changed model/data versions or distributions trigger performance, risk, traceability, and evaluation-scope review.

Inner loop

Check version identity, split integrity, criteria, and result associations.

Outer loop

Inspect population coverage, leakage/independence assumptions, subgroup limitations, and model/build consistency.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture provenance gaps, incorrect version associations, performance failures, and review corrections.

Friction hypothesis

Model, dataset, build, and performance records may be maintained separately.

Measure

Effort to reconstruct the released model/data evidence and unresolved version/provenance gaps.

J15 Improve the working system from corrections and failures
Actor
BBT process owner with project engineers and reviewers
Trigger
Feedback accumulates or an important failure warrants immediate investigation.
Inputs
FB: Correction and failure feedback; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision; RUN: Verification execution and results; DEFECT: Anomaly / defect; CHANGE: Change and impact work; METHOD: Working methods and evaluation evidence
Outputs
FB: Correction and failure feedback; METHOD: Working methods and evaluation evidence; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Retain human edits, rejections, QA/risk comments, missing traces, failures, audit observations, document rework, and agent redirection.
  2. Investigate why patterns recur and distinguish product issues from method or checking failures.
  3. Propose a versioned change to prompts, rules, context selection, tools, or workflows.
  4. Evaluate against relevant failure examples and correct cases, review adoption, and monitor subsequent outcomes.

Decision

The method/process owner adopts a reviewed method version. This does not retrospectively approve product evidence.

Handoff

J01 through J14 use the adopted methods; new project feedback returns to this job.

When something changes

Method changes retain before/after versions and evaluation outcomes; regression or new failures prompt another meta-loop review.

Inner loop

Check feedback identity, source versions, proposed configuration, and evaluation execution.

Outer loop

Independently review whether the proposed method change addresses the cause and introduces new omissions or false alarms.

Feedback to the meta-loop

This is the meta-loop. Regulatory/FDA feedback is one external input; internal project corrections can supply signals from week one.

Friction hypothesis

Repeated corrections may stay in individual reviewers' heads and never improve the process.

Measure

Recurring failure rate, correction effort, and regression outcomes after a method change.

J16 Agree product scope, lifecycle methods, and evidence responsibilities
Actor
Product/project owner with engineering, QA, and relevant domain leads
Trigger
An engagement starts, the toolset changes, or intended use and project scope materially change.
Inputs
SRC: Source revisions; SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; METHOD: Working methods and evaluation evidence; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision
Outputs
SCOPE: Product scope and planning basis; REVIEW: Check, finding, and human decision

Tasks

  1. Agree intended use, users/population, product boundaries, and the evidence activities applicable to this scope.
  2. Agree lifecycle methods, acceptance criteria, review cadence, and responsibilities.
  3. Choose the authoritative home and granularity of each controlled record, including coexistence and handover arrangements.
  4. Record how human and agent work enter the process and who can make each decision.

Decision

The designated engagement owners agree the versioned scope, methods, record ownership, and review responsibilities.

Handoff

J01–J14 use the agreed scope and criteria; J15 uses the agreed method ownership and evaluation approach.

When something changes

Scope, home, method, or responsibility changes prompt review of affected workflows and historical-record access.

Inner loop

Check that the proposed plan names record types, owners, criteria, methods, and unresolved decisions.

Outer loop

Independently inspect whether the planned activities and responsibilities cover the selected product scope and normal developer work.

Feedback to the meta-loop

Capture missing ownership, unsuitable review cadence, migration problems, and avoidable process overhead.

Friction hypothesis

Tool selection may move faster than decisions about record ownership and practical review responsibilities.

Measure

Unassigned decisions, duplicate authoritative records, and effort spent reconciling process or ownership ambiguity.
