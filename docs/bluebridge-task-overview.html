Start with the work colleagues need to do

We used the V-model to identify the jobs, the evidence each job needs or produces, and how that evidence relates. The umbrella tasks below group related jobs under short headings. The matrix shows the capabilities the internal tool would need to support them.

1. V-model

Product development and the evidence that supports it.

2. Jobs and evidence

What people do, which records they use, and where those records belong.

3. Umbrella tasks

Related jobs grouped under a recognisable heading.

4. Capabilities

What the tool needs to help people perform those jobs.

Needed is selected for the starting workflow, including shared prerequisites. Scope is needed when that evidence or activity applies. Addition broadens the workflow later. A dot means no selection in this draft. These labels describe proposed scope, not delivery status.

For example, needs and requirements. Start with text capture, clarification, cited drafting, checking, links and review decisions. Document extraction or live meeting capture can follow. Risk and usability capabilities apply when those records supply requirement inputs.

Internal support means helping people prepare, connect, check and retain evidence. People still conduct engineering and studies, resolve questions and make decisions. To choose the first build, agree a task and a starting scope, then select the capabilities needed to complete it.

Discussion groupings and capability selections are proposals. Evidence homes remain provisional. The detailed basis is in the jobs and evidence map and the feature catalogue.
